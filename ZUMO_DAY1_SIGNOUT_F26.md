# Robot Sign-Out — Fall 2026
### Robotics · D Block · Mercersburg Academy · Mr. Weymuth

> **Print one copy. Fill it in during the first eight minutes of Friday, September 4.**
> This is the only thing on Day 1 that cannot be done later — a student who leaves without a robot
> cannot do Assignment 1.
>
> **The robot is signed out to one student for the whole term** and comes to every class. Bringing
> it, charged, is the Materials grade.

## What the student is signing

Read this line out loud once before they sign. It takes fifteen seconds and it is the whole
agreement:

> **I have this robot, this cable, four batteries and my jumpers. I bring all of it to every class,
> charged. If something breaks or stops working, I tell Mr. Weymuth — I don't hide it and I don't
> try to fix it myself.**

The last clause matters more than it looks. A student who quietly swaps a part, or works around a
dead motor for three weeks, costs a milestone. A student who reports it on the day loses nothing.
